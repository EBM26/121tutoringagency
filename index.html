What we offer

Every student gets a tutor chosen for their subject, level, and the way they learn.

For students and families

Private tutoring
Our tutors are carefully selected and matched to each student’s academic needs and growth.
Remediation
For students who are a few years behind their peers, we assign specialists who meet their needs in a structured setting.
Homeschool support
For parents who homeschool, our team provides an academic environment where students thrive and stay at grade level.
Group classes
A lower-cost option for students who enjoy learning alongside others in a small group.
Online sessions
Remote tutoring for students who prefer to learn online, or when distance makes it the better option.

For schools

Teacher placement
For schools that need to hire staff or cover a classroom, we fully screen and vet qualified educators.

About us

Founded in 2012, 1-2-1 Tutor Agency has worked with hundreds of students, from pre-K through the college level. We place skilled educators face to face with students who want to sharpen their skills or need an extra boost.

We work closely with parents, teachers, and school administrators so every student gets a focused, well-coordinated plan.
